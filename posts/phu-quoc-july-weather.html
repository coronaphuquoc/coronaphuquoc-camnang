July on Phu Quoc: Midseason Rain by the Numbers

July falls in the middle of Phu Quoc's rainy season, which usually runs from May to October. That sounds discouraging on paper, but the actual figures paint a gentler picture than the rain icon on a phone app suggests.

Air temperature

Based on long-term averages, daytime highs reach about 31°C, or 88°F. Nights cool slightly to roughly 24 to 25°C, around 75 to 77°F. Humidity stays high all month, so the air can feel warmer and heavier than the thermometer reading.

Rain behavior

Rain in July usually arrives as short, heavy afternoon showers. Most pass within an hour or two. Mornings are often dry and sunny, which leaves a reliable window for outdoor plans before clouds build.

  • Typical shower timing: afternoon
  • Typical duration: one to two hours
  • Mornings: often dry and bright

Sun and sea

The island still averages roughly five hours of sunshine a day in July. The sea is warm, at about 29°C or 84°F, so swimming remains comfortable, especially in calmer morning conditions.

Reading the forecast correctly

A day marked with rain on a weather app rarely means a full day indoors. It more often means a sunny morning, a downpour after lunch and a clearing evening. Treat the icon as a hint about the afternoon, not the whole day.
