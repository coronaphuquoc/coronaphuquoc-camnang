Radisson Blu on Bai Dai Beach: A Stay Overview

Radisson Blu Resort Phu Quoc stands directly on the sand of Bai Dai Beach, in Ganh Dau ward on the northwest coast of the island. It is a large resort with 514 rooms, suites and pool villas, two outdoor pools and a private beach, which makes it a popular base for families, couples and work travellers alike.

The setting

Bai Dai is known for soft white sand and clear turquoise water. The northwest location feels peaceful, yet the resort remains well connected to the airport and to the attractions clustered in the north, including Grand World and VinWonders.

The building and its style

The architecture mixes contemporary Eastern design with Vietnamese cultural touches. High ceilings and floor-to-ceiling glass let in plenty of natural light. Marble details and artistic accents inspired by lotus motifs and marine life give the public spaces a calm, considered feel.

What the resort offers at a glance

Who it suits

Couples tend to like the rooms with sea views, families appreciate the shallow beach and kids' facilities, and groups can take a villa with several bedrooms and a kitchen. Solo and work travellers find the rooms practical and quiet.
