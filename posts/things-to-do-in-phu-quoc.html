A clear picture of what the island holds

Phu Quoc offers far more than a traditional beach holiday, and understanding its shape helps before you plan. The attractions sit across three main areas. Southern Phu Quoc holds the Hon Thom Cable Car and Sunset Town. Northern Phu Quoc is home to Starfish Beach, Grand World, Vinpearl Safari and VinWonders. Duong Dong and Long Beach form the central area, with convenient access to local markets, restaurants and coastal sunsets. The variety is what stands out when you consider Things To Do In Phu Quoc: a long cable car above the sea, Mediterranean-inspired streets, working pepper farms, wildlife parks and clear tropical waters. Rather than listing everything at once, it helps to picture the island in sections. That way each day has a focus, and you avoid crossing the island repeatedly for scattered stops.
