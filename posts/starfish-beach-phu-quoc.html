Getting to know Starfish Beach on the northwest coast

On the northwestern coast of the island sits Rach Vem, a fishing village that gives Starfish Beach Phu Quoc its setting. The official name is Rach Vem, but visitors use the nickname because of the orange and red starfish resting in the shallow, clear water.

Good to know

At low tide the starfish appear across the sandy seabed, and the scene draws thousands of visitors each year. The shore remains one of the least commercialised spots on the island, so the pace is quiet and the village still runs on fishing.

Practical tips

Three things shape a visit here:

Minh hoạ: starfish beach phu quoc

  • the starfish in knee-deep water near the shore
  • the long wooden bridges running out to floating houses
  • seafood served on raft houses above the water

Conclusion
