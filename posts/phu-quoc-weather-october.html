Phu Quoc Climate in October, Explained Simply

October on Phu Quoc mixes warm sunshine with brief tropical showers. The island is still in its rainy season, yet the month has a calmer, softer feel than the wettest weeks of summer, and the crowds of high season have not yet arrived.

Temperature and humidity

Temperatures usually sit between 27°C and 29°C, a steady warmth that rarely feels extreme. Humidity stays fairly high at around 76 percent, so light clothing and regular water breaks help.

Rainfall

Rain is frequent. Averages point to more than 20 rainy days and roughly 246 mm of precipitation over the month. Long-term figures vary between datasets, so treat these as a guide rather than a promise. The encouraging part is that most showers are short, and plenty of sunshine hours remain each day.

What that means on the ground

  • Beach plans and boat tours can still be affected by rain.
  • Morning or post-shower hours are the most reliable for outdoor time.
  • The landscape is lush and green after months of rain.
  • Evenings often feel a little cooler and more comfortable.

Before you fly

Check the local forecast a few days before travel and again on arrival. Keep at least one indoor activity in mind for each day so a shower never leaves you without a plan.

Conclusion

October brings warmth, regular but short rain and a quieter island. Knowing the numbers helps you set realistic expectations and plan days that make the most of the sunny spells. Further reading: October temperatures and rainfall on the island.
