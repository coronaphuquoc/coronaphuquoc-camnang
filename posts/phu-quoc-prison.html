Two Sessions a Day: Timing Your Phu Quoc Prison Visit

The Coconut Tree Prison in the south of the island does not keep shop hours that run straight through the day. It opens in two sessions, and travellers who ignore that split often end up waiting outside the gate in the midday heat.

The morning and afternoon windows

The site opens daily from 8:30 to 11:30 AM, then closes for lunch, and reopens from 1:30 to 5:00 PM. There is no admission charge and no ticket queue to account for, but there is also no way to slip in during the break.

  • Morning window: three hours, from 8:30 AM.
  • Lunch closure: 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM.
  • Afternoon window: three and a half hours, until 5:00 PM.

How much time you actually need

Most visitors spend one to two hours covering Zone B2, the memorial, the exhibition house and the graveyard. A guided tour adds roughly 30 to 45 minutes. That means arriving at 10:45 AM is risky, because the lunch closure will cut your visit in half.

Why the first hour works

Tour groups from resort packages tend to arrive between 10:00 and 11:00 AM. If you reach the gate before 9:30 AM, the quieter first hour gives you room to read the signage and stand at the memorial without crowds. The drive from Duong Dong takes about 30 to 40 minutes, so leaving around 8:30 AM is sensible.

Using the afternoon instead

If your morning is taken, aim for a 1:30 PM arrival. The outdoor areas have limited shade, so bring a hat and sunscreen, especially between April and October.
