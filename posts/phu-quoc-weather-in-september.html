Phu Quoc in September at a Glance

September falls in the peak of the rainy season on Phu Quoc. Days are hot and humid, showers and thunderstorms come often, and the sea can turn rough for stretches at a time. It is not a washout month, though. Sunny spells still appear regularly, and the island looks lush after weeks of rain.

The numbers in brief

  • Temperature: roughly 25 to 30°C through the month.
  • Humidity: around 85 percent, so the air feels heavy.
  • Rainfall: on average somewhere between about 225 mm and 257 mm.
  • Showers: usually short and tropical, mostly in the afternoon.

How the days tend to unfold

The key feature of September is how quickly things change. A bright morning can turn into a thunderstorm within a few hours, and a grey start can clear by midday. Rather than reading this as unpredictable in a bad way, treat it as a reason to keep plans loose and have an indoor option ready.

The sea

Periods of rough sea are part of the month. That affects boat trips and swimming more than anything else. Beaches on the sheltered southern side, such as Sao Beach and Khem Beach, generally stay calmer than exposed stretches.

Who it suits

September works for travelers with flexible plans who are happy to trade a few beach hours for quieter surroundings and green scenery. It is harder for anyone who has booked fixed boat or beach days.

Conclusion

Think of September as hot, humid and changeable, with plenty of rain but no shortage of bright moments. A flexible plan is the single most useful thing you can bring. Further reading: September rainfall and sea conditions.
