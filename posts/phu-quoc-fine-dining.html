Securing a Special Dinner Table in Phu Quoc High Season

Between November and April the island fills up, and the dining rooms that people plan a whole evening around fill up with it. A refined dining room with set menus and a view cannot, so the people who book ahead are the ones who get the table they wanted.

Which meals need a booking

Casual local places rarely take reservations, and the advice there is simply to arrive early, since popular bowls often sell out before noon. The picture changes for romantic venues, upscale restaurants and the dining rooms inside the large northern resort complexes, where booking in advance is strongly recommended during peak months.

  • Book the anniversary or birthday dinner as soon as flights are fixed.
  • Ask for a sea view or a fountain view at the time of booking, not on arrival.
  • Confirm opening hours directly, since some venues ask guests to contact them for current times.
  • Keep one evening unbooked as a buffer for weather or a late flight.

Fitting the dinner around the day

Plan the special meal on a day spent nearby. If you are exploring Grand World or the northern theme parks, a dinner in the Bai Dai area means a short ride rather than an hour across the island. If the day is spent in the south around Sunset Town, choose a table there instead and save the northern dining room for another night.
