April on Phu Quoc: The Numbers Behind a Gentle Month

April is the hinge between two seasons on Phu Quoc. The dry months are ending, the southwest monsoon has not yet settled in, and the island sits in a quiet pocket of warm, mostly sunny days. If you like to plan with figures rather than guesses, this month is easy to read.

Temperature range

Daytime readings run from about 25°C to 33°C, which is roughly 77°F to 91°F. Early mornings hover around 26 to 28°C and feel fresh enough for a walk along the sand. By noon the thermometer can pass 32°C, so the middle of the day is when most locals slow down and stay in the shade.

Rain and humidity

Total rainfall for the month usually lands somewhere between 60 and 80 mm. For context, the wettest months of the year can see 300 mm or more. In April, showers tend to arrive in late afternoon or evening and rarely last longer than half an hour.

  • Humidity starts near 75% in early April
  • It climbs to around 80 to 85% by the end of the month
  • Short rains often bring a small, welcome drop in humidity

Sea and daylight

The southwest breeze is light, typically 5 to 10 km/h, and the water around the An Thoi Archipelago stays calm with visibility of 5 to 8 meters. You get about 12 hours of daylight, with sunrise near 5:50 AM and sunset close to 6:10 PM.

Conclusion

Read together, these figures describe a month with warm water, calm seas and only brief rain, plus a hot midday stretch to plan around.
