Feeding Young Children at Avenue Breakfast in Phu Quoc

Parents staying at Radisson Blu Resort Phu Quoc often worry about the first meal of the day. Small children wake early, get hungry fast and can be picky. Avenue Restaurant, the main dining room on the resort grounds, opens its breakfast buffet at 7:00, which suits families whose kids are up with the sun.

What small eaters tend to pick

The buffet has a Western side and an Asian corner, and both hold dishes that work for children. A quick scan before you fill a plate saves wasted food.

  • Bread, cereals and cheese for kids who want something familiar
  • Eggs cooked the way you ask, so a plain scrambled egg is easy to get
  • Porridge from the Asian corner, soft and mild for toddlers
  • Fresh fruit juices if your child does not drink milk at home

Pho and dim sum are there too, and older kids who like noodles usually head straight for them.

Timing tips for a calmer morning

The first hour after opening tends to feel relaxed. Families who come down around 7:00 can pick a table near the floor-to-ceiling windows, where children can look out at the garden and pool while they eat. If your little ones nap late in the morning, remember the buffet runs until 11:00, so there is room to come back for a second, smaller round of fruit before it closes.

Room category matters

Breakfast is included with many room types, but not all. Check your booking confirmation before you go down, so there are no surprises at the table. The landing page also lists a separate child rate for kids under 12; ask the front desk for the current figure, since rates can change.

Conclusion

A good breakfast sets the tone for a beach day, and Avenue gives parents enough choice to keep both toddlers and teens fed without leaving the resort. Further reading: Avenue dining guide with meal hours.
