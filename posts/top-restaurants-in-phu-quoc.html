Mixing Your Own Bowl of Bun Quay in Duong Dong

Bun quay is the one island dish that asks the diner to do some of the work. The name comes from the stirring: you build your own dipping sauce at the counter, then carry it back to a bowl of fresh rice noodles, shrimp paste and hand-ground fish cake served hot.

The sauce formula

At Bun Quay Kien Xay the self-service station follows a fixed set of ingredients: kumquat, sugar, salt, pepper and chili. Staff will show newcomers the usual order, and the trick is to taste as you go rather than tipping everything in at once.

  • Squeeze the kumquat first, since its sourness sets the base.
  • Add sugar and salt in small pinches and stir hard.
  • Pepper from the island goes in next, then chili to your own limit.
  • Taste with a strand of noodle before adding more of anything.

Which branch to choose

The main branch sits at 28 Bach Dang in Duong Dong Ward and is listed as open from 7:00 AM to 11:45 PM. A second branch is at 71A Tran Hung Dao, and the original shop is in the alley opposite 222 Tran Phu, usually trading in the evening from about 5:00 PM. There is also a branch in the Shanghai Quarter of Grand World for visitors staying further north.

Beating the queue

Peak hours run roughly 8:00 to 10:00 AM and 6:00 to 8:00 PM. Arriving just outside those windows means a quicker seat and more room at the sauce station. A bowl is listed at around 50,000 to 100,000 VND, though prices can change.

Conclusion

Treat the first bowl as practice and the second as your own recipe. It is a cheap, filling breakfast that teaches more about local taste than any menu description. Further reading: island eating guide by cuisine and area.
