When the Dry Months Arrive on Phu Quoc

Phu Quoc sits in a tropical monsoon zone near the equator, so it stays warm all year. What changes is the rain and the state of the sea, and that split defines the two seasons visitors plan around.

The basic calendar

  • Dry season: November to the end of April, with clear skies, bright sunshine and calm water.
  • Rainy season: May to October, when the southwest monsoon brings frequent showers and stronger waves.
  • Average temperature across the year: around 25 to 27°C.

Inside the dry months

The window is not uniform. December to February is the peak, both the driest and the coolest part of the year. March and April form the shoulder: hotter, less crowded, and the last reliable stretch for beach days before the monsoon returns.

Why the sea matters

During the dry months the sea is calm, with wave heights under half a metre. That is what keeps boat trips to the southern islands, snorkelling tours and kayaking running without weather interruptions. Visibility underwater reaches roughly 8 to 10 metres for snorkellers.

Island breezes

Minh hoạ: phu quoc dry season

Because the island is surrounded by sea, breezes keep the air fresher than on the mainland, even when the midday sun is strong.

Planning around the season

Since this is the busiest time on the island, flights and accommodation fill early. Booking well ahead gives you more choice, especially for Christmas, New Year and Tet.

Conclusion

If your trip revolves around beaches, boats and sunsets, aim for November to April and refine the month by heat and crowds. Further reading: month-by-month dry season weather guide.
