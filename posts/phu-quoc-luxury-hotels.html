Where High-End Stays Cluster Around Phu Quoc

Phu Quoc is long and narrow, and the upscale resorts are not spread evenly along it. They gather in three areas, each with a different personality. Knowing which zone suits you narrows the choice faster than reading endless reviews.

The north coast around Bai Dai

The northern beaches host large, lively resorts close to Grand World and the safari and theme park area. Radisson Blu Resort Phu Quoc has 514 rooms and villas on the beachfront, and Wyndham Grand Phu Quoc next door has 1,399 rooms and villas in a European-style setting. VinHolidays Fiesta sits inside Grand World itself with 687 compact, modern rooms.

  • Good for travellers who want evening entertainment within walking distance.
  • Easy access to night markets and family attractions.
  • Large pools and wide stretches of sand.

Long Beach on the west coast

Long Beach runs south of the north cluster and is known for sunset views. Regent Phu Quoc, InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort and Salinda Resort all sit here, along with La Veranda, inspired by a 1920s French mansion. This stretch suits couples and anyone who wants to watch the sun drop into the Gulf of Thailand.

Khem Beach in the south

Khem Beach is quieter, with fine pale sand. JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay, designed by Bill Bensley around a university theme, and the all-villa New World Phu Quoc Resort, modelled on a fishing village, are both here.
