Staying in a Villa at Wyndham Grand on Bai Dai: An Overview

The villa wing of Wyndham Grand Phu Quoc sits on the Bai Dai coast in Ganh Dau, on the quiet northern side of the island. It belongs to a resort of 1,399 rooms and villas, yet the villas are planned around a simple idea: privacy inside a shared resort. You get your own walls, garden and pool, while the restaurants, spa and beach are a short stroll away.

Where it sits on the island

Bai Dai is a long, calm beach on the northwest coast. The resort is roughly 27 km from Phu Quoc International Airport, which most drivers cover in about 35 minutes depending on traffic. Grand World, VinWonders and Vinpearl Safari are all in the same northern cluster, so day trips stay short.

What kinds of stays are on offer

The property runs two broad tiers. Inside the main building are Superior and Deluxe rooms of 42 m², with garden, pool or ocean views and a private balcony. Outside, standalone villas range from two and three bedroom garden layouts to beachfront houses with a private plunge pool.

  • Garden villas: two or three bedrooms, set among tropical planting
  • Beachfront villas: direct sand access and a plunge pool
  • President Beachfront Villa: six bedrooms for large groups

Who tends to choose a villa

Families with grandparents along, groups of friends sharing a kitchen table, and couples who want a quiet base all fit the villa format. If your trip is mostly theme parks and markets, a regular room may cover what you need.

Conclusion
