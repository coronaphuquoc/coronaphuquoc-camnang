January on Phu Quoc: The Driest Weeks Explained

January sits in the heart of the dry season on Phu Quoc, and locals often call it the driest month of the year. If you are deciding between several winter dates, the January profile is worth understanding in detail before you book.

Temperature and daylight

Days usually peak between 28 and 31°C, while nights settle at a gentle 23 to 25°C. Daylight lasts around 11 to 12 hours, and roughly 8 of those are direct sunshine. That gives you long, bright mornings for the beach and comfortable evenings for walking around town.

  • Daytime: about 28 to 31°C
  • Night: about 23 to 25°C
  • Sunshine: around 8 hours a day

Rain and air

Rainfall is minimal. When a shower does come through, it tends to be light and brief, then the sky clears again. Humidity is relatively low, so the air feels fresh rather than heavy, and a sea breeze takes the edge off the afternoon heat.

Sea conditions

The water stays near 27 to 29°C, with gentle waves and clear visibility. That combination suits swimming, snorkeling and diving across the island, from the southern archipelago to the quieter west coast. Early mornings tend to be the glassiest time on the water, before any afternoon breeze ruffles the surface.

Conclusion

January stands out for its reliability: steady sun, mild nights, low humidity and a calm sea. The trade-off is that many other travelers have noticed the same thing, so plan your bookings around high demand and keep the hottest midday hours for shade or a long lunch.
