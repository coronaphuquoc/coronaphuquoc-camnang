Inside Avenue Restaurant at Radisson Blu Phu Quoc

Avenue is the main dining room of Radisson Blu Resort Phu Quoc in the Bai Dai area on the north-west coast of the island. If you are staying at the resort, or simply passing through the north, it is worth knowing how this restaurant works across the day before you sit down.

The setting

The room is contemporary and bright. Floor-to-ceiling windows look out over the gardens and the swimming pool, so natural light pours in at breakfast and the space feels open at lunch. In the evening the mood turns warmer and quieter. The layout suits several kinds of meal: a family gathering, a working lunch or a dinner for two.

What the kitchen cooks

The menu blends international cooking with island flavours. In the morning you find a large buffet with a Western side and an Asian corner. At midday, Asian and European dishes sit side by side, from salads and sandwiches to rice, noodles, pasta and grilled steak. At night, the focus moves to fresh seafood, grilled plates and Phu Quoc specialities, with a seafood buffet on selected days.

How service feels

A team of professional chefs plates each dish with attention to colour, texture and balance, and staff work to international service standards. Requests for special diets are taken seriously.

Who it suits

  • Resort guests who want three meals without leaving the grounds.
  • Visitors from nearby hotels looking for a reliable sit-down meal.
  • Couples planning a calm dinner after a day at the beach.

Conclusion
