Getting to Sao Beach from Duong Dong

Duong Dong, the island's main tourist hub, is the usual starting point. The beach sits about 25 km to the south, in Bai Sao Hamlet near An Thoi, and the drive takes roughly 30 to 40 minutes.

Good to know

Motorbike, taxi and private car are the common ways to make the trip.

Practical tips

Minh hoạ: sao beach

The popular route heads south from the town centre along 30 Thang 4 Street, then continues on Nguyen Van Cu Street (DT46), the main road towards An Thoi. Keep an eye out for signs marked "Bãi Sao" and turn onto the narrow side road that leads to the entrance. A short walk past beachfront restaurants brings you to the shore.

Conclusion

Parts of the access road are narrow and uneven, especially in the rainy season, so riders should go slowly. For more, see the how to reach Sao Beach on Phu Quoc.
