March on Phu Quoc: How the Dry Season Ends

March is the closing chapter of the dry season on Phu Quoc. The island is still sunny and settled, but the days run a little hotter than in the cooler weeks around the turn of the year. Knowing the profile helps you plan where to be at each hour.

Temperature range

Expect temperatures between about 25 and 32°C, or 77 to 90°F. Sea breezes keep the afternoons from feeling overwhelming, and evenings settle into a pleasant warmth suited to walks and outdoor dinners.

  • Range: about 25 to 32°C
  • Humidity: around 76 percent
  • Monthly sunshine: close to 250 hours

Rainfall

Average rainfall is only about 50mm for the month, which places March among the driest months of the year. When showers come, they are usually brief and light and often fall at night, so they rarely interfere with daytime plans.

Sun and sea

With nearly 250 hours of sunshine across the month, clear days are the norm. The sea is typically calm, clear and warm, which suits swimming, snorkeling and diving from the southern islets to the long west coast.

What it means for planning

The hotter middle of the day is the main thing to plan around. Put active outings in the morning, keep shade or indoor time for early afternoon and return to the water later in the day.

Conclusion

March offers warm, sunny days, little rain and calm water as the dry season winds down. Respect the midday heat and you will find it an easy month to enjoy. Further reading: a detailed March forecast for the island.
