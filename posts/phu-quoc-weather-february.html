February Climate on Phu Quoc at a Glance

February is deep in the dry season on Phu Quoc, and its numbers read like a checklist for a beach holiday. If you are weighing it against neighbouring months, the key difference is how low the humidity falls.

Temperatures

The average sits close to 28°C. Afternoons can reach around 31°C, and evenings ease to about 23°C. That is warm enough for long swims yet cool enough after dark to enjoy a walk without feeling sticky.

  • Average: about 28°C
  • Highs: around 31°C
  • Lows: around 23°C

Rain and humidity

Rainfall reaches its yearly minimum, with only a slight chance of a short shower passing through. Humidity drops to its lowest point of the year. This is what makes the heat feel pleasant: sweat evaporates, the breeze feels fresh, and long days outdoors are less tiring than in the wet months.

Sea temperature and clarity

The water averages around 28°C and the surface is calm and clear. Swimming, snorkeling and diving are comfortable around much of the coast, from the northern beaches down to the southern islets.

How it compares

Compared with the wetter half of the year, February brings steadier sunshine and drier air. For most visitors, the main thing to manage is sun exposure rather than weather risk.

Conclusion

Low humidity, warm days, mild nights and a calm sea define February on the island. Keep sunscreen and water close and leave the rain gear at home, though a compact layer for evenings is still sensible. Further reading: the full February weather overview.
