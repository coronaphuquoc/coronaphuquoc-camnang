What to pack and what to avoid at Khem Beach

A little preparation makes a day on Phu Quoc Khem Beach smoother. Pack these basics:

  • swimwear, a towel and sun protection
  • a hat and shade for the middle of the day
  • a dry bag for phones if you plan to kayak or paddleboard
  • cash for loungers, rentals and food

Good to know

A few mistakes come up often. Some visitors walk onto private sections of the bay; the designated public area is at the eastern end, so head there first. Others arrive at midday and meet both crowds and harsh sun, when an early start between 5:30 and 7:30 AM would be calmer.

Practical tips

Swimming outside lifeguard hours is another issue. Lifeguards are present at the public area from 8 AM to 5 PM, so plan swims within that window, especially with children.
