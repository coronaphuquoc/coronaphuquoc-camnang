Buying fish sauce wisely

Some factories offer guided tours at no charge, though details vary, so check on arrival. Your main spending decision is what to take home.

Good to know

Read the label before choosing. The protein rating, shown in °N, tells you the strength: 40°N and above means premium first press. Lower grades are fine for cooking. Look for a clear, amber colour, sometimes described locally as "cockroach wing colour", with no cloudiness.

Practical tips

A few habits help:

Minh hoạ: phu quoc fish sauce factory

  • taste several grades before buying, then choose for how you will use it
  • buy a small quantity of the premium grade and a larger cooking bottle if you cook often
  • ask about vacuum packaging if the sauce is going in your luggage

Conclusion

Because the protected name in European markets is reserved for sauce made on the island with traditional methods, buying at a Phu Quoc fish sauce factory lets you see where your bottle comes from. Further reading: spending wisely around a Phu Quoc fish sauce workshop.
