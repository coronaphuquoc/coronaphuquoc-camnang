Photographing Ong Lang's coves and sunsets

The bay offers two main subjects: its rocky coastline and its sunsets. For calm water and soft light, shoot between 7 AM and 9 AM, when the sea is flattest. A kayak opens up small coves to the north, though keep cameras in a dry bag.

Good to know

For sunset, the rocky outcrop at the northern end gives a view about 3 to 4 metres above the waterline. Arrive 30 minutes before the sun goes down to claim a position, as the beach fills from around 4:30 PM.

Practical tips

Underwater, visibility is clearest between November and March, when currents are minimal. Parrotfish and damselfish live around the northern rocks, so a waterproof camera can capture reef life.

Conclusion

Mixed textures help: golden sand, dark rock, seaweed and emerald water. These elements give images of Ong Lang Beach, Phu Quoc Island a sense of place without heavy editing. Further reading: photography tips for Ong Lang Beach.
