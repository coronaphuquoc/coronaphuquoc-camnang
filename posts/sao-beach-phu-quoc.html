Night Squid Fishing Trips Near Sao Beach

Most visitors leave the south of Phu Quoc once the sun drops, yet the evening is when the fishing boats around An Thoi come alive. If you are spending a day at Sao Beach, a night squid trip is an easy way to stretch the outing into something you will talk about for years.

How the trips work

Boats depart from An Thoi port, a short drive from the Sao Beach turn-off. After dark the crew switches on bright lamps that pull squid toward the surface, and guests drop simple lines over the side. Daytime departures exist too, built around rod fishing in the open breeze.

  • Typical cost on the source guide: 400,000 to 600,000 VND per person, though rates can change
  • Many trips bundle return transport, fishing gear and a seafood meal cooked on board
  • Ask the operator which pickup point applies to your hotel

Fitting it around a beach day

Spend the morning swimming at Sao Beach while the water is calm, eat lunch at one of the beachfront seafood places, then rest through the hottest hours. Head to An Thoi in the late afternoon so you are on the pier before departure rather than rushing along Nguyen Van Cu Street at dusk.

Minh hoạ: sao beach phu quoc

  • Bring a light long-sleeved layer, as the sea breeze cools quickly at night
  • Pack motion-sickness tablets if you are sensitive to swell
  • Keep a dry bag for your phone, since spray reaches the deck

Conclusion

A night on the water shows a working side of the island that daytime beach visits miss. Pair it with a slow morning on the white sand and you have a full southern day without long drives.
