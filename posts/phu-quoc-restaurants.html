A Noodle Soup Walk Along 30/4 Street in Duong Dong

Some of the island's most loved bowls sit within a short walk of each other on 30 Thang 4 Street in Duong Dong town. If you only have one morning in the centre, you can taste three different broths without needing a taxi between them.

Start early with fish broth

Kèn Út Lượm at 42 30 Tháng 4 Street serves bún kèn, a thick golden soup made from ground fish such as barracuda or yellowtail scad, blended with coconut milk and a light curry note. The listed hours run from 6:00 AM to noon, and the pot often runs out before closing, so treat it as your first stop rather than your last.

Squid broth at the same address

Hủ Tiếu Mực Tư Hường is listed at the same 42 30 Tháng 4 address. The broth is simmered from bones and fresh squid, so it tastes clean and lightly sweet. It is a gentler bowl if the coconut soup felt rich.

Finish with crab

Minh hoạ: phu quoc restaurants

Quốc Anh Crab Noodle Soup is also on 30/4 Street. Its brick red broth and thick noodles make it the heaviest of the three, which is why it works as the closing bowl of the walk.

  • Bún kèn: around 35,000 VND a bowl
  • Squid noodle soup: around 40,000 to 70,000 VND
  • Crab noodle soup: around 40,000 to 80,000 VND

These figures come from the guide and can change, so carry small notes and check the board at the counter. Share bowls between two people if you want room for all three.

Conclusion

A short street, three broths, and a pace set by when the pots run dry. Walk it before the midday heat and you will understand why locals eat soup for breakfast. Further reading: island eating guide by cuisine and area.
