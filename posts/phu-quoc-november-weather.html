November on Phu Quoc: The Month the Seasons Change

November is the month when Phu Quoc turns from wet to dry. Monsoon rain becomes steadily rarer, sunshine takes over and the large crowds of the peak season have not yet arrived. For many travelers that makes it a sweet spot between the two seasons.

How the transition feels

Early mornings often start with a light mist that lifts to clear skies. Showers still happen, but they tend to be short, often 20 to 30 minutes, and they usually come late in the afternoon or in the evening. By the middle of the month, conditions generally settle further, which suits outdoor plans.

Key figures

  • Air temperature: about 24 to 30°C (75 to 86°F).
  • Sea temperature: around 28°C (82°F).
  • Rainfall: roughly 200 to 250 mm over 12 to 15 days.
  • Humidity: 75 to 85 percent, eased by coastal breezes.
  • Sunshine: about 6 to 7 hours a day.
  • Wind: around 10 to 15 km/h.

Early versus late November

The first half of the month can still feel like the tail of the rainy season, with more frequent showers. The second half usually brings steadier weather. If your dates are flexible and outdoor activities matter most, the later weeks are the more reliable choice. Travelers who come early can still enjoy plenty of sunshine, but should leave a little room in the schedule for a passing shower.

Conclusion

November is a month of transition that leans clearly toward the dry season. Expect warm days, warm water, short showers and softer light, with conditions improving as the weeks go on. Further reading: how the island shifts into the dry season.
